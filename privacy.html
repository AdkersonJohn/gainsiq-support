GainsIQ — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

GainsIQ is a workout tracking app that runs on your device. It does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or usage data. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-in exists.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included.
  • Your data never leaves your device. Your workouts, exercises, templates, and schedule are stored only in a local database on your device.
  • No third parties receive your workout data or any personal information.
  • Deleting the app deletes your data.

Exercise demo photos

Some exercise demonstration photos are downloaded from the jsDelivr content delivery network (cdn.jsdelivr.net) the first time you view them, then kept on your device. Like any web request, this lets jsDelivr see your device's IP address and standard request details, as described in jsDelivr's privacy policy. No workout data or personal information is included in these requests.

Exercise data and photos come from free-exercise-db (public domain).

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. If you have questions, contact john.adkerson.software@gmail.com.
