GainsIQ — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 14, 2026

GainsIQ is a workout tracking app that runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or usage data. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-in exists.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included.
  • No data leaves your device. Your workouts, exercises, templates, and schedule are stored only in a local database on your device.
  • No third parties receive any information.
  • Deleting the app deletes your data.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. If you have questions, contact john.adkerson.software@gmail.com.
